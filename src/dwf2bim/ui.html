dwf2bim

Enter a block and parcel in Tel Aviv. This gets the newest permit documents from the city's engineering archive and prepares a model for Revit.

A browser window opens on the archive. Read its terms of use and approve them there. The documents are not certified copies, and some are protected by copyright.
